07. Technical & Operational FAQ
06 / QUESTIONS & CLARITY
Straight Answers from Our Engineering Desk.

Do we need to replace our existing CRM, ERP, or internal software?
Almost never. Our primary goal is to connect and automate the tools your team already uses—whether that is HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Tally, SAP, Google Workspace, or custom databases. We only recommend new software if your current setup lacks basic data access.

What happens if an automation or AI agent encounters something unexpected?
Every production system we build includes a defined failure and escalation path. When an input is incomplete, ambiguous, or outside confidence thresholds, the system pauses, logs the context, and routes the item to a designated human review queue rather than guessing.

Why do you use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) instead of fine-tuning for company knowledge?
Fine-tuning bakes static information into a model, which becomes outdated the moment you update a price list or policy—and still risks hallucination. RAG acts like an open-book reference system: every time a question is asked, the system retrieves the latest approved document from your files and cites the exact source.

How secure is our internal company and customer data?
We deploy via private enterprise API endpoints or within your own cloud environment (AWS, GCP, Azure). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, governed by role-based permissions, and never shared with public model training pools.

How long does a typical AI workflow or agent build take?
A focused single-workflow automation or WhatsApp lead concierge typically moves from audit to production in 3 to 5 weeks. Multi-system enterprise RAG cores or complex multi-agent pipelines typically take 6 to 10 weeks. Timeline and scope are locked before build begins.

What do we need to prepare before booking a Workflow Audit?
Nothing formal. Simply identify one repetitive process or bottleneck that costs your team time—who is stuck doing it, which software tools are involved, and what a better outcome looks like.
